Hoop Climb

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the Hoop Climb mobile application, published by rlsdeveloper89, handles information.

Information stored by the app

Hoop Climb does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or payment information. The app stores your selected ball style and best score locally on your device so that these choices can be retained between play sessions.

Advertising

Hoop Climb displays banner advertising through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interaction information, and diagnostic information. This information may be used to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize advertising, subject to your device settings, your choices, and applicable law.

Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Internet connection

Hoop Climb requires an active internet connection to operate. The connection is used to load banner advertisements and related advertising services.

Sharing of information

The developer does not sell personal information or directly provide your locally stored game score or selected ball style to third parties. Information processed by Google AdMob is handled by Google and its partners as described in their policies.

Your choices

You may use the privacy, advertising, and reset controls available in your Android device settings. Availability and effect of these controls can vary by device, Android version, region, and advertising partner.

Security

This app doesnot collect any personal data. All records are stored locally in user's phone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its advertising services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact rlsdeveloper89 through the Hoop Climb listing on Google Play.
